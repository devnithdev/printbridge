PrintBridge

Print from any phone or computer to any printer in the office.

This installs PrintBridge on this Mac. Near the end, a short page opens in your web browser and asks what this Mac does: the main computer, a computer that shares a printer, or one that only prints. The installer window waits while you answer, so it may look paused. The browser page shows a progress bar (LibreOffice, if you choose it, is a download of about 300 MB).

You will be asked for an administrator password.
